## 29. Ambiguities: decisions still needed from you

_(Defaults in italics are what I'll assume if you don't say otherwise, for the TBD items below.)_

### Decided

1. **Vercel plan and the terms risk.** Proceed on Hobby, risk accepted. Re-confirm with the owner before launch (§26 step 21); upgrade trigger stays first Vercel warning or first real revenue.
2. **Account ownership.** Every account is created and owned by the business owner: domain, Razorpay, **and now also Cloudflare, Vercel, Supabase, Resend and GitHub** (reversing the earlier draft where the last five were agency-owned). The developer manages them day to day via invited team-member access or scoped API tokens/keys, not shared credentials — see §27. This removes the continuity and DPDP-Act data-control risk entirely, since nothing depends on the developer's own accounts surviving or staying reachable.
3. **Business/legal — GST.** **No GSTIN — not GST-registered.** Below the registration threshold is legally normal for a small business, but confirm with an accountant whether/when registration becomes required. Consequence: `store_settings.gstin` stays empty, `tax.ts` and the invoice template must work with GST fields blank (no fabricated GSTIN/HSN on invoices), and this needs resolving before **Razorpay live-mode KYC**, since Razorpay's document requirements depend on business type — check with Razorpay directly whether a non-GST-registered sole proprietor/individual can activate live payments and what documents (PAN, bank proof, etc.) substitute for the GSTIN.
4. **Catalogue at launch.** Larger catalogue, 50+ products. Doesn't change the architecture, but two consequences: (a) Phase 11's admin product-creation UI is one-product-at-a-time by design (§13) — at 50+ products that's meaningfully slower than the small-catalogue case the phase estimate assumed, so budget extra admin data-entry time, or revisit adding a CSV/bulk-import tool (currently not planned, easy to add later without a redesign); (b) whether real photos/copy will be ready by Phase 12 (content & polish), or whether placeholders are needed for longer, is still open — see TBD #4/#14.
5. **Who uses the admin panel.** Owner + one more admin (e.g. a helper). Matches the existing design exactly: `role` is just `customer`/`admin`, no per-staff permissions, so this needs 2 admin accounts (§13) — no scope change. (2FA was scoped, built, then deliberately removed in Phase 11; see §27's "Admin lockout" row.)
6. **Payment-pending hold time.** **5 minutes.** Trade-off: UPI app-switching and bank OTP can take longer, so expect more "paid just after expiry" cases, handled by the reconciler (re-reserve, or refund if the stock is gone).
7. **Contact-form destination mailbox and sender address.** Placeholder for now. Wired as config (`CONTACT_INBOX_EMAIL`, `EMAIL_FROM` in §19), not hardcoded, so swapping in the real addresses later is an env-var change, not a code change. Needs to be a real, monitored inbox before launch — contact-form submissions and order emails are otherwise silently unread.

8. **Paid-order cancellation:** _admin-only (the customer contacts support)._ **Confirmed by the owner (2026-09-26):** customers can't cancel an order once it's placed — the storefront gets **no** cancel button, on paid or unpaid orders. The backend's customer path for backing out of an **unpaid** checkout (`cancelPendingOrder`, `pending_payment → cancelled`, docs/06 §10) stays built but unused by the UI — unpaid checkouts expire on their own after the 5-minute hold and show as "Not paid" in the admin.
